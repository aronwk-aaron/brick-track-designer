Laying track

  • Click a grid point or a track end to start; move to see the track; click to lay it.
  • Esc or right-click stops drawing.
  • Start on the middle of a track to branch off it.
  • Drag a track's end to reshape it; drop it on another end to join them. Esc while dragging puts it back.

View and keys

  • Wheel zooms; right/middle-drag or Space+drag pans.
  • Select: click a track; Shift+click adds more; Ctrl+A all. Ctrl+C/X/V copy, cut, paste (at the mouse), Ctrl+D duplicate, M mirror, R / Shift+R turn 90°.
  • D draw, S select, Del delete, F fit, P 3D preview, Ctrl+Z undo.
