Brick Track Designer

Click a grid point or a track end to start, move to see the track, click to lay it; right-click or Esc stops. Drag a track's end to reshape it; drop it on another end to join them. Start on a track to branch off it. Right/middle-drag or Space+drag pans, wheel zooms. flangeway cut check rail, circle = rail crossing, square = points (not built yet)
